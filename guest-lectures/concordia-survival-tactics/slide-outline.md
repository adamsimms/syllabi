## Identity — *concept → map → findability (5 slides + a divider)*
5. **What is a virtual identity?** — the sum of everywhere you show up online (site, socials, search results, listings, and what *others* post); not just what you publish but what people **find**; for an artist, an **extension of the practice** — another format the work lives in, like a show or a book; you can't fully control it, but you can **shape** it.
6. **Why it matters** — people meet your work — and you — **online first**, often when you'll never know; curators/jurors/galleries/press look you up before shows, grants, residencies; the **connective tissue** your CV + applications point to; absent/scattered reads "not serious," coherent builds **trust**.
7. **Public, private, or mixed?** — decide **practice-facing vs. personal**; three models (all-public / split accounts / curated blend); weigh boundaries, safety, and how much process/life is visible; consistent *identity* (name, handle) ≠ oversharing — **control the aperture**.
8. **The Ecosystem** *(studio vs. group show folded in)* — **home base = your website** (your studio; you own it; the full permanent record); **outposts = everywhere else you appear** (social **plus** links to your site, press, exhibition texts, gallery/directory listings, interviews); **audience = your mailing list** (reach directly, any platform); outposts point back to the hub, the hub turns visitors into audience. *Native diagram.*
9. **How people find & vet you** — they search your name + follow CV/press/listing links; be **consistent** (name, headshot, bio voice → one person); claim your **@handle** everywhere, own a **domain + pro email**; make the work easy to find and **you easy to contact**. *Real Google-results screenshot for your name.*

## Files / Documentation — *full tables in [file-formats.md](file-formats.md); consider a printed cheat-sheet handout*
22. **Documenting any medium** — photo, sculpture, video stills, installation shots. *Good vs. bad doc example.*
23. **Print → web shift** — inches+DPI → **pixels**; TIFF → JPEG/WebP; Adobe RGB → **sRGB**; 16-bit → 8-bit.
24. **Image files for the web** — JPEG (q 70–80) / WebP / PNG / SVG; longest edge **~2400 px** for full-bleed; the "72 dpi myth." *WebP saves natively in PS; AVIF via Squoosh.app. Cargo caveat: it accepts JPEG/PNG/GIF/SVG only (no WebP/AVIF) — export sRGB JPEG for the demo.*
25. **Video for the web** — MP4 (H.264) is the safe universal; 1080p is plenty; captions; muted autoplay loop vs. click-to-play; a still frame for the poster.
26. **Host it, don't self-host** — embed from **Vimeo** (clean, private, swappable) or **YouTube** (reach); big self-hosted files kill load time. *Cargo embeds both.*
27. **Alt text & accessibility** — describe your work; helps SEO too.
28. **One site, every screen** — phone (~390px) / tablet (~768px) / desktop (~1440px+); **mobile-first**; builders are responsive by default but check every breakpoint; test on a real phone. *Device-size visual.*
29. **Go deeper** — link to PHOT 332 site. *QR code.*
