### Segment 3 — Developing a style talking points
- Start from the **feeling**: minimal/index ↔ immersive/experimental (deeper in Inspiration) — let the work decide.
- Choosing a palette: **[Coolors](https://coolors.co)** (fast generate/lock/export, free, no account) as the default. Artist move: **[Adobe Color → "Extract Theme"](https://color.adobe.com)** to pull a palette *from your own artwork* so the site echoes the work. Optional live preview: **[Realtime Colors](https://realtimecolors.com)**.
- Keep it minimal — 2–4 colours + one type pairing is plenty. Restraint reads as confidence.

### Segment 4 — Inspiration talking points
- The web is a **format**, with its own affordances — like an exhibition, a book, or a zine. Each holds work differently.
- Your site is a **container that extends and expresses the work** — give it the same intention as hanging a show or sequencing a book.
- "What kind of website does *your* work want to be?" Minimal/index ↔ immersive/experimental. The design itself is a statement.
- Where to look: artists' sites you admire + **Cargo's featured/curated** gallery. Study *choices* (nav, pacing, typography, how images breathe), not just looks.

### Segment 7 — video notes
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264) is the universal safe bet; WebM as an alt. 1080p is plenty for web — 4K is heavy and rarely needed on screen.
- **Host it, don't self-host:** embed from **Vimeo** (artist-friendly: clean player, no ads/recommendations, password + privacy controls, swap the file without changing the link) or **YouTube** (reach + search). Large self-hosted files slow your site and burn bandwidth. Cargo embeds both.
- **Playback intent:** muted autoplay loops for ambient/background pieces; click-to-play with sound for main works. Prefer a short looping MP4/WebM over a heavy GIF.
- **Accessibility & polish:** add captions/subtitles; export a still frame as the poster/thumbnail.
- Time-based, performance, and installation work all live here — video is how most non-photo media travels online.

### Segment 7 — screen-size notes (phone / tablet / desktop)
- **Mobile-first:** most visitors arrive on a phone. Design for the small screen first, then scale up.
- **Three rough targets:** phone (~390px), tablet (~768px), desktop (~1440px+). Don't chase exact numbers — design for the ranges.
- **Builders are responsive by default** (Cargo, Squarespace) — but *always* check every breakpoint. Common failures: tiny tap targets, hero text that overflows, nav that breaks.
- **Test on a real phone** before publishing. In the demo, show Cargo's mobile preview + per-breakpoint tweaks.

## Before class — prep checklist
- [ ] Have your real content ready to paste: artist statement, short bio, 1–2 projects + statements, CV, and a few web-sized images.
- [ ] Pre-build a demo Cargo account (fallback if live signup/wifi fails), pre-loaded with the same content.
- [ ] Pre-load browser tabs: 3–4 curated artist sites, Cargo featured gallery, Coolors, Adobe Color, your own current site, the demo Cargo editor.
- [ ] Cargo code(s) ready to hand out.
- [ ] Your own site pre-loaded to open full-screen for the opening "studio visit."
- [ ] Intro-slide assets dropped into the deck: a headshot + 2–3 of your strongest works; a screenshot of your site as a hook-slide backup.

**On SEO/domain (Segment 10):** keep it practical — your name in the page title, alt text on images; buy a domain (~$12/yr) and set up email forwarding to a pro-looking address. Not a deep dive.
